Project 03 · Automation / EOAT

Gear-Driven Parallel Gripper Assembly

Mechanical end-of-arm tooling with a central drive pinion and synchronized dual spur gears operating a parallel linkage system and serrated jaws for secure automated part handling.

Design objective

Provide robust synchronous closure and uniform gripping-force distribution for robotic automation cells.

Key features

  • Dual spur gears and central pinion drive
  • Four-bar linkage for parallel jaw motion
  • Serrated jaw faces and flanged robotic mount

Engineering considerations

Kinematic binding avoidance, gear-mesh tolerancing, grip-force transmission, and pin-joint shear-stress management.

CAD & manufacturing

Kinematic mechanism modeling, gear constraints, assembly management, and motion simulation; suited to machined aluminum or steel with precision CNC or COTS gears.
