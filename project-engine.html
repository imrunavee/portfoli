Project 02 · Powertrain assembly

Engine Assembly with Induction System

V-configuration engine assembly integrating cylinder-head covers, intake piping, twin turbocharger/impeller components, front pulley and belt system, and exhaust manifold hardware for packaging and interference review.

Design objective

Integrate induction, exhaust, and accessory-drive components into a complete engine assembly for packaging and interference verification.

Engineering considerations

Component interference, thermal zoning between intake and exhaust, belt alignment, pulley centre distance, and engine-bay packaging.

CAD skills

Large multi-part assembly modeling, mate/constraint management, sub-assembly integration, and belt/pulley mechanism modeling.

Engineering value

Demonstrates complex powertrain assembly management across mechanical, thermal, and motion interfaces.
