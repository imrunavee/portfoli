Project 01 · Powertrain / aerospace

Turbofan Jet Engine Cutaway Assembly

A full-length, sectioned two-spool turbofan assembly exposing fan, compressor, annular combustor, turbine stages, and nozzle architecture for clear engineering review.

Design objective

Communicate internal gas-path flow, rotor/stator stage arrangement, and system integration in a complete turbomachinery assembly.

Key features

  • Multi-stage axial fan and compressor
  • Annular combustor and fuel-manifold hardware
  • High- and low-pressure turbine stages

CAD skills

Large rotating assemblies, circular aerofoil patterns, clipped section views, nested-spool mates, and appearance assignment.

Engineering value

Shows multi-stage aerospace gas-turbine architecture with legible internal-system visualisation.

Interactive model preserved from the supplied eDrawing export.
